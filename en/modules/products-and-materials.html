Products & materials

Your catalog and inventory, connected to production.

Register every product with its specs and every material with its type, unit and stock. Everything stays connected to the BOMs, routings and orders that use them.

What’s included

Designed to be understood at a glance.

Materials inventory

Stock in sight, shortages before they stop the plant.

Raw materials, consumables, dies and packaging with stock, level versus minimum and how many BOMs use them. Import materials from a file.

  • Filters by type, category, class and status
  • Specifications per material
  • Where used in BOMs
Products

The master sheet of every product.

SKU, technical specs, versions, linked customers, BOMs, routings, documents, generated orders and completed units.

  • Technical versions
  • Commercial SKUs per customer
  • Units requested and completed
Master catalog

Search, filter and sort your products. From the sheet you reach its BOM, its routing and every order ever made.

FAQ

Questions about the products & materials.

Can I import my current materials?

Yes, with “Import from file” you upload your materials list and the system registers it.

Which specs can I record?

Whatever your product needs: dimensions, color, finish, gauge, compatibility, cavities and more.

Are products linked to customers?

Yes, with commercial SKUs and linked customers on the product sheet.
