Documents & PDF orders

The complete order, in a PDF ready to sign.

Every production order generates its document: product, customer, sales order, BOM, routing, manufacturing sequence with times and signatures, and materials required versus consumed.

Request a demoExplore modules
What’s included

Designed to be understood at a glance.

Sequence & signatures

Every step with its time and signature.

The PDF shows the manufacturing sequence with type, machine, quantity, start, end and signature space. Download, print or share it.

  • Order summary and planning
  • Materials required, issued, consumed and scrap
  • Key specs of every material
Documents

Every file, linked to what it belongs to.

Orders, customer POs, datasheets, artwork and quality certificates, each linked to its order, sales order, customer, product or material.

  • Folders by document type
  • Search by document, order or customer
  • Drag and drop files
Traceability

The full story of every order.

From the moment the order was confirmed to the last signature: who created the order, when it was scheduled, who verified materials and who closed each step.

  • User, date and time on every event
  • Useful for audits and customers
  • Evidence ready for claims

How it works

In four steps.

  1. The order is created

    With its BOM and routing.

  2. It is produced and signed

    Each step on the display.

  3. The PDF is generated

    With real data.

  4. It is archived

    Linked to the order and sales order.

FAQ

Questions about the documents & pdf orders.

Does the PDF fill itself?

Yes. It takes data from the order, the BOM, the routing and the operators’ logs.

Which file types can I upload?

PDF, images and spreadsheets, among others: artwork, datasheets, customer POs and certificates.

Can I share the PDF with my customer?

Yes, it works as manufacturing and lot traceability evidence.
