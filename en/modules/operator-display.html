Operator display

Your operators run production from a tablet.

Each operator sees only what they need: the current step, the machine, the materials to verify and how much has been produced. When done they sign and the order moves to the next step.

Request a demoExplore modules
What’s included

Designed to be understood at a glance.

Step materials

Before producing, materials are verified.

The display lists that step’s materials with quantity and specs. The operator marks each one ready and the system confirms “Materials verified”.

  • Required quantity and specifications
  • Ready status per material
  • View of the product being made
Logs & signature

Sign and continue. That simple.

Every action is saved in the step log with date, time and user. Closing the step, the operator signs and the order moves on.

  • Start, end and logged time
  • Produced versus planned quantity
  • Previous step / View next

How it works

In four steps.

  1. Open the order

    From the list or by number.

  2. Verify materials

    Mark each one ready.

  3. Log production

    Pieces and step notes.

  4. Sign and continue

    The order moves on.

FAQ

Questions about the operator display.

Does it work on any tablet?

Yes, it runs in the browser on iPad, Android or Windows. Nothing to install.

Does each operator have a user?

Yes, so every log and signature is under the person who did it.

What if a material is missing?

The order can be marked blocked and it appears in the dashboard alerts.
