Customers & sales orders

From the customer’s order to the production order.

Register your customers and their orders, and turn each line into a production order. Sales knows how far along every order is without asking the floor.

What’s included

Designed to be understood at a glance.

Customers

Everything about each customer on their record.

Active orders, linked products, documents, deliveries, claims and cumulative output. With legal name, tax ID, currency, payment terms, contacts and addresses.

  • Contacts by department
  • Shipping and billing addresses
  • Currency and payment terms
Sales orders

Every order knows how far along it is.

Order source, customer PO, date, requested delivery, priority, currency and status. The Production tab shows linked orders and their progress.

  • Lines with quantity, price and delivery
  • Statuses: draft, confirmed, in production, delivered
  • Order documents

How it works

In four steps.

  1. Register the customer

    Data, contacts and addresses.

  2. Capture the order

    With its lines and PO.

  3. Create the POs

    One per line.

  4. Track it

    Progress visible to sales.

FAQ

Questions about the customers & sales orders.

Can one line create several orders?

Yes, you can split the quantity into several orders based on capacity or dates.

Can I work with pesos and dollars?

Yes, every customer and order has its currency.

Can sales see progress without entering production?

Yes, the order shows the progress of every linked production order.
